About Our Adventures

Learn more about what makes Green Peak the ultimate adventure experience in Munnar

We Create Unforgettable Experiences

Nestled amidst the tranquil hills of Munnar, Green Peak offers a unique blend of nature, adventure, and comfort. More than just a campsite, our property is designed to provide a memorable experience for every traveler — whether you're seeking relaxation, exploration, or both.

We offer spacious tents (accommodating 4 people each), a rustic mud house with two rooms (8-person capacity per room), and a cozy wooden cabin — all surrounded by nature’s beauty. The property also includes essential amenities like a resting area, campfire space, three clean and well-maintained bathrooms, and a nearby kitchen that serves three wholesome meals a day — breakfast, lunch, and dinner.

Your journey begins at 3:00 PM from town, with an adventurous ride to the property. After arrival at 4:00 PM, guests are welcomed with snacks, followed by evening activities including a lively campfire, music, and dance.

At 6:00 AM, embark on a thrilling trek to Chokramudi Peak, returning by 9:30 AM for breakfast before heading back at 11:00 AM via jeep. The property also boasts a scenic viewpoint perfect for sunrise moments and peaceful reflection.

With comfortable accommodation, hygienic toilet and bathroom facilities, delicious food, and exciting adventures — Green Peak is where your spirit of adventure meets the soul of Munnar.
